Professional Application Guide

Master the deca-strength bond with our precision-engineered workflow.

01

Surface Preparation

Ensure the surface is clean, dry, and free from dust or grease to promote maximum polymer anchoring.

02

Mixing Process

Mix with water to a smooth, lump-free consistency. Let stand for 5 mins to activate chemical bonds.

03

Even Application

Apply evenly using a notched trowel. For large slabs, ensure 100% coverage on both tile and substrate.

04

Placement & Set

Press tiles firmly. Adjust alignment within the 45-min open time. Allow to dry fully before grouting.

The Origin

Why We Entered the Race

In 2014, our founder Hafiz Muhammad Ahmed identified a critical flaw in Pakistan's construction materials: adhesives were being treated as mere fillers rather than structural anchors.

PowerPak was established in Islamabad to challenge these low standards, engineering polymer-modified solutions specifically designed for extreme durability.

The mission was clear: to set a benchmark so high that PowerPak became the only name synonymous with structural safety.

10+ Years of Defiance

Industrial Evolution

From the Capital to the Industrial Heart

2014 - 2019

The Islamabad Genesis

PowerPak was forged in the shadows of the Margalla Hills. What began as a specialized laboratory focusing on high-end polymer science soon became a challenge to the status quo. In Islamabad, we perfected the formulas that would eventually redefine Pakistan's construction standards.

2019 - Present

The Lahore Expansion

To serve a growing nation, we moved to the industrial epicenter: Lahore. Here, we transitioned from a boutique lab to a high-capacity manufacturing fortress. Our current facility represents the pinnacle of adhesive technology, fueling the skyline with 10X and RK Bond.
